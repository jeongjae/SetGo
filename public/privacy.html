SetGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

SetGo is a local-first workout logging app. It is designed to help you record strength training, cardio, routines, and progress without requiring an account or a hosted SetGo backend.

Information SetGo Stores

SetGo stores your workout data locally on your device. This may include exercises, routines, workout sessions, sets, weights, repetitions, cardio entries, notes, preferences, app settings, and locally generated analysis.

No Account or SetGo Server

SetGo does not require you to create an account. SetGo does not operate a server that collects your workout data, and the app does not upload your workout history to a SetGo cloud service.

Local Storage

Your data is stored on your device using local app storage. If you delete the app, clear browser/app data, reset your device, or remove local storage, your SetGo data may be deleted unless you have made your own backup.

Backups and Exports

SetGo may let you export or back up your data. Exported files are under your control. If you save, send, upload, or share an exported file, the privacy of that file depends on the destination you choose.

Optional External AI Coach Endpoint

SetGo may include an optional coaching feature that can use an external AI endpoint configured by you. If you enable and configure that feature, relevant workout information and prompts may be sent to the endpoint you provide so that a coaching response can be generated. SetGo does not control the privacy practices of external services you choose to use. Review the privacy policy and data handling terms of any external endpoint before enabling it.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

SetGo does not include third-party advertising. SetGo does not sell your personal information. SetGo does not use your data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children's Privacy

SetGo is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through SetGo, contact us so we can review the issue.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as SetGo changes. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
